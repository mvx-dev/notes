The behaviour of light cannot be purely explained by assuming it is fully defined prior to measurement. This can be experimentally tested by a formulation by Clauser-Horne-Shimony-Holt (CHSH) (Clauser et al. 1969), which was adapted from a problem defined by Bell (1964), which itself was also adapted from an argument put forward by Einstein et al, (1935). This works by measuring the polarisation of photons that have been entangled using a specific crystal (forgot the name), which has statistics that cannot be explained using classical models of locality, realism, and free will.
