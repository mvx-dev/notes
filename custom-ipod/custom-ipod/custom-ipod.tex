\section{Design Brief}
\subsection{Overview}
The primary objective of this device is to design and implement a portable, battery-powered digital audio player capable of high-fidelity playback of lossless audio formats. This design will focus on only local playback of audio files, rather than streaming from cloud services.

This device will be implemented using an STM32 microcontroller, and will focus on repairability, transparency, and reproducibility.

\subsection{Motivation}
Most portable audio players are either a smartphone with compromised audio stages (due to other requirements of those devices), or premium digital audio players that are expensive and proprietary. This project aims to demonstrate and construct a purpose-built embedded platform that can achieve comparable objective performance using modern components while remaining open and reproducible.

\subsection{Objectives}
\subsubsection{Primary Objectives}
Below are the primary objectives of the device.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item It should be portable and battery-powered
    \item It should be capable of playing back lossless audio formats
    \item It should have a high-dynamic range
    \item It should have low output noise
    \item It should have low harmonic distortion
    \item It should have support for low- to mid-power headphones and audio devices
    \item It should have long battery life
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Secondary Objectives}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item It should have the ability to function as a USB DAC
    \item It should have expandable storage
    \item It should have easy to update firmware
    \item It should be completely open-source
    \item It should be completely user-repairable
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Requirements}
\subsubsection{Functional Requirements}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The device shall be capable of playing all popular audio formats
        \begin{enumerate}
            \item WAV
            \item FLAC
            \item MP3
        \end{enumerate}
    \item It shall support pulse-control modulation (PCM) up to at least 24-bit/192 kHz
    \item It shall decode files from removable storage
    \item It shall maintain playback location between sessions or after power loss.
    \item It shall display metadata about the currently playing file
        \begin{enumerate}
            \item Title/Artist/Album
            \item Duration/position in file
            \item Album cover/file icon
        \end{enumerate}
    \item It shall provide hardware volume control
    \item It shall provide information on the state of the device
        \begin{enumerate}
            \item Battery monitoring
            \item Storage capacity and usage
        \end{enumerate}
    \item It shall utilise the current standard connectors/ports where applicable
        \begin{enumerate}
            \item The charging and data transfer shall use a USB Type-C connecction
            \item The audio output shall use a standard 3.5mm audio jack.
        \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Performance Requirements}
The device shall have at minimum the following specifications
\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \caption{Performance requirements for the device}
    \label{tab:1}
    \begin{tabular}{l|c}
        Parameter & Target\\
        \hline
        \hline
        Frequency response & 20 Hz-20 kHz $\pm 0.2$ dB \\
        THD+N (total harmonic distortion) & $<0.001\%$ \\
        Dynamic range & $>120$ dB \\
        SNR (signal-to-noise ratio) & $>120$ dB \\
        Crosstalk & $<-100$ dB \\
        Output impedance & $<1\Omega $ \\
        Headphone output & $>500$ mW @ $32\Omega $ balanced \\
        Idle battery life & $>20$h \\
        Playback battery life & $>10$h
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}

\subsubsection{Interface Requirements}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The interface shall match or exceed the follow specifications of the iPod Classic 7th Gen display
        \begin{enumerate}
            \item 2.5in (63.5 mm) (diagonal) colour LCD with LED backlight
            \item 320x240 pixel resolution at 163 PPI
        \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Design Considerations}
\subsubsection{Audio Integrity}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The device shall separate analogue and digital ground planes with a controlled connection
    \item The device shall isolate high-speed digital traces from sensitive analogue circuitry
    \item The device shall use differential routing for DAC outputs where appropriate
    \item The device shall minimise loop areas and return-path discontinuities
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Power}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The device shall have separate analogue and digital regulators
    \item The device shall have low-noise references
    \item The device shall have high PSRR LDOs
    \item The device shall have sufficient decoupling
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{EMC}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The device shall shield crystal oscillators
    \item The device shall have controlled impedance USB routing
    \item The device shall have ground stitching around analogue sections
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Verification}
In order to verify device performance, the following tests must be conducted
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Frequency response
    \item THD+N
    \item SNR
    \item Dynamic range
    \item Crosstalk
    \item Output impedance
    \item Maximum output power
    \item Battery runtime
    \item Power consumption
    \item Thermal performance
\end{enumerate}
