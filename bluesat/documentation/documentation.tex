\section{PR Detector}
Atmospheric depth
\[
    X=\int_h^\infty\rho(h')dh'
\]

Hydrostatic equilibrium
\[
    \frac{d P}{d h}=-\rho g
\]

Atmospheric depth in terms of pressure
\[
    X=\frac{P}{g}
